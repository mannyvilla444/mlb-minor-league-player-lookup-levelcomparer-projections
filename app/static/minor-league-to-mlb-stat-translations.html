Research approach

The researcher reviewed historical career stats for players in the reference list, identified approximately 55–60 players with the lowest MLB playing-time totals who had played in each respective minor league exclusively, and averaged the observed difference between that league’s numbers and MLB numbers.

The stated research focus is current MLB players with roughly 300–2,000 career plate appearances. The full reference list below should not be read as a list of qualifying observations for every league; per-league sample membership, exact sample sizes, dates and weighting were not provided.

How to apply a translation

For a player from one of these leagues, add that league’s mean change to the player’s minor-league stat as a simple baseline: estimated MLB stat = minor-league stat + league mean Δ.

Example: a Pacific League AAA hitter with an .850 OPS would translate to about .648 OPS using the supplied −.202 mean. A 10.0% BB rate would translate to 6.7% using −3.3 percentage points.

This is a descriptive league average, not a validated player-level forecast. Playing time, season environment, age, park, selection effects and the player’s own skill can change the result. OPS and SLG are related, so do not subtract both changes from OPS.
